Can we fix it?

Things we can't take, and why
  • Gas appliances — they need a registered gas engineer.
  • Microwave ovens and tube (CRT) TVs — they store dangerous voltage.
  • Swollen or damaged lithium batteries — fire risk.
  • Large appliances (washers, fridges, ovens) — they don't fit our tables.
  • Firearms, airguns and weapons, and medical devices.

House rules

  • Repairs are free. Donations to cover parts and tea are welcome but never required.
  • You stay with your item; we don't keep items overnight.
  • One booked item per person per session.
  • Fixers are volunteers and can't guarantee a repair.
  • We don't stock spare parts; the fixer writes down what to buy.
  • Cancel at least 24 hours ahead so the slot can go to someone else.
